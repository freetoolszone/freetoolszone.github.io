Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Absolutely! All conversion happens in your browser. Your images never get uploaded to any server.

How many images can I convert?

You can convert up to 20 images at once. Each image must be under 5MB in size.

What image formats are supported?

We support JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP image formats.

Does it work offline?

After the first load, the tool can work offline since everything runs in your browser.
